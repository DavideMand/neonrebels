Intro / Cardano NFT Collection

Neon Rebels

In Neon City, six diverse allies-Blaze, Riot, Kaiden, Ivy, Juno, and Luna-unite against oppression, navigating digital chaos and towering skyscrapers. Together, they challenge corruption, symbolizing hope for a liberated future, their collaborative synergy becoming beacons of change in a city submerged in urban turmoil.

001 / Character

ブレイズ - Blaze

Born to a family of brilliant programmers, Blaze's electric hair symbolizes her fiery determination. After a corporate scandal shattered her family, she immersed herself in hacking, becoming Neon City's digital phantom.

002 / Character

会田 - Kaiden

Raised in the slums, Kaiden honed stealth skills to survive. Rumors swirl around her cryptic past, fueling her motivation to dismantle the city's corruption. Her affinity for disguise and subterfuge makes her an enigmatic yet crucial member of the team.

003 / Character

ルナ - Ivy

From a lineage of innovators, Ivy's inventions rival the city's elite technology. Her heart beats for the marginalized, each creation a step towards equality. In her workshop, surrounded by tools and blueprints, she crafts hope for a better Neon City.

004 / Character

暴動 - Riot

A victim of an industrial accident, Riot emerged with cybernetic strength. Her fearless nature and sense of justice made her the city's guardian. Beneath her tough exterior is a compassionate soul, fighting for the downtrodden, her cyber enhancements a testament to her resilience.

005 / Character

ジュノ - Juno

A strategist since childhood, Juno's mind deciphers chaos into plans of action. Her leadership keeps the team cohesive in the face of danger. Behind her calculating gaze lies a fierce loyalty to justice and her comrades.

006 / Character

アイビ - Luna

Gifted with empathetic senses, Luna feels the city's heartbeat like a second pulse. Her intuitive nature binds the group, offering solace amidst turmoil. Her unwavering empathy kindles the flame of revolution, illuminating a path to a brighter future.

007 / Character

セレナ - Selena

Detective Selena Cruz was once an idealistic recruit, then jaded by Neon City's corruption. Tasked with hunting the rebels, she battles inner turmoil, torn between duty and conscience.

008 / Character

マラ - Mara

Sergeant Mara Kincaid rose through the ranks and embodies authority. With a ruthless determination to maintain order, she pursues the rebels, her loyalty unwavering.

009 / Character

ミア - Mia

Patrol Officer Mia Vasquez is entrusted with the city's streets. Torn between loyalty to her comrades and empathy for the rebels, she grapples with her conscience.

010 / Character

ダックス - Dax Orlan

Driven by integrity, Dax fights to dismantle corruption and empower Neon City's oppressed. His efforts inspire hope, but his ideals put him in the crosshairs of powerful enemies.

011 / Character

エララ - Elara Voss

A shrewd politician thriving in Neon City's shadows, Voss manipulates policies to benefit corporations. Her charisma masks greed, making her a dangerous force against rebellion and reform.

012 / Scene

禁断の接続 - Forbidden Connection

A quiet rupture in Neon City's controlled system: intimacy, signal, and synthetic life meeting inside the machinery of the city.

013 / Scene

ロボットファクトリー - Robot Factory

The production floor behind the myth: polished shells, industrial silence, and the systems that manufacture obedience.

Plot / 01

Six unlikely allies emerge against a dystopian backdrop.

In the sprawling metropolis of Neon City, Blaze wove through the digital realm like a phantom. Riot stood as protector. Kaiden danced in the shadows. Ivy arose as a brilliant engineer whose inventions rivalled the city's technology.

Plot / 02

They become beacons of change.

Juno devised plans with calculated precision. Luna understood the city's pulse like a living being. Together, they bypassed surveillance, resisted oppressive regimes, exposed hidden corruption, and fought for a future where freedom could thrive.
